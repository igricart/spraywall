Privacy policy

Spray Wall is a free, non-commercial app for sharing climbing routes on a gym spray wall. This page explains what it stores. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

What is stored

  • Routes you save: route name, grade, the holds, the feet rule and the setter name you type. These are public to everyone using the app.
  • If you sign in with Google (only needed to log sends): your Google account id, name and email address, as provided by Google.
  • Sends you log: which route, your optional grade vote and star rating. Totals (number of sends, consensus grade, average stars) are public; your name is not shown next to them.
  • On your device only: preferences such as Font/V grade scale and your setter name, kept in the browser's local storage.

What is not done

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, no cookies beyond what sign-in needs.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

You can remove any send yourself in the app. To delete your account and all your sends, open an issue at github.com/igricart/spraywall/issues or ask the gym staff, and it will be removed.
